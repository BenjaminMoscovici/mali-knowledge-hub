### Onboarding contract and next scope

An approved registry entry can declare annual observations with localized labels and notes, measure and definition identifiers, geographic identity, unit, aliases, a release ID and version, and rows with year, audited value, record ID, source locator and original excerpt. Aliases describe the measurable subject and place. Use the same geographic identity and definition only when the rows really are comparable. The adapter binds the public source URL from the registry. Activation for this iteration is limited to exact annual lookup and comparison, with relative year follow-ups. No automatic spreadsheet ingestion or generalized cross-topic synthesis is claimed.

Next, qualify a second real source family through the same contract, then compare broader discovery from registry capability metadata against the existing research path. Measure cited answer quality, usable-answer rate, time to verify, wall time and provider cost on paired professional tasks. Use an isolated environment with model credentials and a read-only corpus to qualify hosted answers before promotion.
